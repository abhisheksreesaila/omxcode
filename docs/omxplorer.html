Speed

The window never waits.

Big queries run beside the window, not in it. The longest pause while 100 million rows are crunched is two frames.

1.8sopen 100M rows from Parquet
0.77smedian by region over 100M rows, on the CPU
33msthe longest the window paused meanwhile
0.6msa chart over 1M rows, grouped and sorted
0.3msthe status bar's sum and average, 1M rows
0.8msa frame of 1M-row grid scrolling, p95

The island

One capsule tells you everything.

at restthe file, its rows, the GPU
runningwhat, how far, how fast
donehow long it took, and where
undowhat changed, one tap back
errorstays until you've seen it

It rests small. When something happens it grows to say what, in 320 ms on a calm curve, then settles back. Tapping it opens your recent work and the GPU's timings.

The look

Liquid, on every screen.

One docked shell, and cards that tile like windows: a new one splits the space, a closed one gives it back, none ever overlaps. Liquid dark, and Ledger, Graphite and Blueprint a tap away. The same app, natively drawn.

framework

omxcode

Native controls on each platform, the GPU canvas, the agent tools.

engine

A dataframe engine in Mojo: SQL, joins and group-bys on the GPU or the CPU, queries as background jobs.

The design language: tonal surfaces, one soft shadow, calm motion.
