# Product Hunt: the listing (omxplorer)

**Launch when:** `[clip: robots-45s]` is recorded and omxlabs.tech is live with a way to try or download omxplorer
(see ../no-excuses-fund/live-checklist.md). Earliest: Thu Nov 5 or Tue Nov 10. Not on a weekend.

**Name:** omxplorer

**Tagline** (≤ 60): `Sheets, video and documents on your own GPU, agent-ready`

Alternatives:
- `Why did it fail? Ask your data, on your own GPU`
- `A spreadsheet that plays the video behind every row`

**Link:** https://omxlabs.tech · **Also:** https://github.com/abhisheksreesaila/omxcode

**Description** (≤ 260):

```
omxplorer opens a sheet, its video and its documents side by side, joined only by links you make. Pivot, click a row, watch what happened on one time cursor, ask in words. It runs on your own GPU, never freezes, and every button is an agent tool.
```

**Topics:** Developer Tools, Data & Analytics, Robots, Artificial Intelligence, Open Source

**Pricing:** Free (open source, MIT)

**Video (YouTube link):** YouTube 1, "Why did the robot drop it?" (or `[clip: robots-45s]` uploaded unlisted)
