# Product Hunt: launch-day tips

**The day before**
- Schedule the launch for **12:01 am Pacific** (PH's day starts then). Tuesday to Thursday has the most traffic; Friday and the weekend have less competition. Pick one and don't move it.
- Check the link works on a phone. PH visitors will tap "Visit" from their phones; omxlabs.tech must load, and must say plainly how to try omxplorer (download for Mac/Linux, or the hosted version).
- Prepare the first comment (first-comment.md) and the 9 gallery images (listing.md).
- Make a short list of people who've already replied to you on X or the forum. Tell them the day, not "please upvote": PH penalises vote asking. "We're live on Product Hunt today, I'd love your feedback" is fine.

**On the day**
- Post the first comment at once.
- Post on X with the PH link and `[clip: robots-15s]` (template below), and add a line to the forum thread.
- Reply to every comment within the hour, all day. Answers from the reply bank (../modular-forum/replies.md) work here too.
- If someone finds a bug, thank them, say when you'll fix it, and fix it that day if you can. Then reply with the fix.

**X post for launch day**

```
omxplorer is live on Product Hunt today.

A spreadsheet, its video and its documents on one page, on your own GPU. Click a robot's failure and its video plays next to its telemetry, on one cursor.

Open source, built in Mojo. Feedback welcome: [PH link]
```

**After**
- Add "Featured on Product Hunt" to omxlabs.tech only if it gets featured.
- Note the day's numbers (visits, comments, GitHub stars before/after) in docs/launch/kit/README.md for the No Excuses Fund's traction answer.
