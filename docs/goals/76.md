# GOAL 76: a tabbed ribbon; the side panels closed until asked

**End goal (owner, 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18):** "having a ribbon for all of them where you sort of hide the side panel, the
inspector… put in the insert button at the top"; "what about ribbon? closing out panels (side etc.) and moving the left side to
the top". Asked to pick from two mockups, the owner chose **a tabbed ribbon** (Home · Insert · Data · Format · View, each tab a
band of grouped, labelled buttons; the menu bar merges into it) and **docked panels, closed by default** (the inspector, Links
and the other right-hand panels open from their ribbon button and the cards re-tile narrower; closed, the cards get the width).
