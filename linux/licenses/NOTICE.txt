omxplorer for Linux carries four runtime libraries from Modular's Mojo and MAX packages,
byte for byte as Modular ships them (not modified), in lib/:

  libKGENCompilerRTShared.so    from mojo-compiler 1.1.0
  libAsyncRTRuntimeGlobals.so   from mojo-compiler 1.1.0
  libMSupportGlobals.so         from mojo-compiler 1.1.0
  libAsyncRTMojoBindings.so     from max-core 26.6.0
