omxplorer for Mac (the GPU build) carries four runtime libraries from Modular's Mojo
and MAX packages, byte for byte as Modular ships them (not modified, not re-signed),
in OmxplorerMojo.app/Contents/Frameworks:

  libKGENCompilerRTShared.dylib    from mojo-compiler 1.1.0
  libAsyncRTRuntimeGlobals.dylib   from mojo-compiler 1.1.0
  libMSupportGlobals.dylib         from mojo-compiler 1.1.0
  libAsyncRTMojoBindings.dylib     from max-core 26.6.0
